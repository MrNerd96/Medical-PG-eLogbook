# NMC rules implemented in the MBBS (UG) e-logbook

## Sources (official)
- **NMC, Guidelines for preparing Logbook for Undergraduate Medical Education Program** (Expert Group; for MBBS from 2019-20):
  <https://nmc.org.in/storage/new/Logbook-Guidelines_17.01.2020.pdf>
- **NMC, Competency Based Medical Education (CBME) Curriculum 2024**, issued 12 Sept 2024, for the 2024-25 admission batch onward
  (copy: <https://medicaldialogues.in/pdf_upload/cbme-guidelines-2024-250434.pdf>).
- Graduate Medical Education Regulations (GMER-2023) / GMER 2019 Part II clause 11.1.1.b.7: submission of the logbook is a
  prerequisite for the final summative exam.

## Logbook format (NMC guidelines)
Each logbook table has 8 columns, reproduced in **Competency Log** and the printed logbook:

| # | Column | Values |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Competency # | Subject code + number, e.g. `PY6.8`, `AN2.1` |
| 2 | Name of activity | e.g. "Spirometry", "Seminar on liver" |
| 3 | Date completed | dd-mm-yyyy |
| 4 | Attempt | **F** first/only · **R** repeat · **Re** remedial |
| 5 | Rating | **B** below · **M** meets · **E** exceeds expectations, or a numerical score |
| 6 | Decision of faculty | **C** completed · **R** repeat · **Re** remedial |
| 7 | Initial of faculty & date | Recorded in the app with the **Verify** action |
| 8 | Feedback received | Initial of learner |

- Separate pages for the Foundation Course, AETCOM, Early Clinical Exposure, integration, humanities, each subject,
  sports and extracurricular activities. One logbook per phase, submitted before the exam.
- Ends with a **Final Summary** (attendance %, complete/incomplete, teacher's signature) and a **Logbook certificate**
  signed by the faculty, countersigned by the HOD and the Principal/Dean. Both are in *Print Logbook*.
- Print or electronic format is left to the institution; CBME 2024 calls an e-logbook "desirable".

## CBME 2024 requirements

| Requirement | App section |
|---|---|
| Phase I 12 mo (AN, PY, BI, intro CM), Phase II 12 mo (PA, MI, PH, FM, clinical), Phase III-1 12 mo (FM, CM, ENT, OPH exams), Phase III-2 18 mo (Medicine, Surgery, OBG, Paediatrics + allied) | Profile → current phase; dashboard per phase |
| Foundation Course (2 weeks), **75% attendance mandatory**, certified by the Principal/Dean | Foundation Course |
| Early clinical exposure in Phase I | Early Clinical Exposure |
| AETCOM in every phase (modules 1.1–1.5, 2.1–2.8, 3.1–3.5, 4.1–4.9); **75% attendance needed for all university exams** | AETCOM |
| Family Adoption Programme: **at least 3 families (5 preferred)**, **80% attendance in family visits** | Adopted Families, Family Visits |
| Clinical postings, 80% attendance in each; end-of-posting assessment; written case records incl. outpatients; submitting the case record/logbook is needed for the subject's final exam | Clinical Postings, Case Records |
| Electives: 2 blocks of 15 days in Phase III-2, **75% attendance + elective logbook** needed for the final exam/NExT | Electives |
| Attendance: **75% theory, 80% practical/clinical** in each subject, in each phase it is taught | Attendance |
| Internal assessment: **≥40% theory and ≥40% practical separately, ≥50% combined**; cumulative across phases; ≥3 IA exams in Phase I & II subjects, ≥2 in Phase III subjects | Internal Assessment → dashboard eligibility |
| Logbook (incl. skill certifications) carries 10–20% of IA marks | Rules page |
| Certifiable competencies for the phase must be completed and logged before the subject's university exam | Competency Log (certifiable flag) + per-subject targets |
| Mentor–mentee programme, 1 mentor : 3 mentees, allotted in the Foundation Course | Other Activities → Mentor meeting |
| IA split across phases: Medicine 50/50/100, Surgery 25/25/150, FMT 25/75, Community Medicine 25/25/50, ENT & OPH 25/75 | Allied subjects roll up into parent subject IA |

## To set locally
- The **list and number of certifiable competencies** per subject is decided by the college/university; enter them in
  *Targets & Backup*.
- AETCOM module titles differ by institution, so students type the topic against the module number.
